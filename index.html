Jarvis 2

Jarvis 2 is a private, personal assistant used by one person (Omri Dahan) on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Jarvis 2 reads its owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail, and creates calendar events and email drafts, only when he asks it to.

  • All processing happens on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Google access tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on that computer only.
  • Email is never sent automatically; Jarvis 2 can only create drafts.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Jarvis 2's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
